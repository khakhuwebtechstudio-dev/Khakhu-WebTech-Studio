THE PERSON BEHIND THE VISION

Building Ideas Through Technology.

Khakhu WebTech Studio is a digital development business founded by Todani Khakhu, with a vision of making modern digital solutions accessible to businesses and individuals.

Based in Gauteng, in the East Rand of South Africa, the studio focuses on website development, web applications, e-commerce experiences and creative digital solutions.

Our approach is built around understanding each project's purpose, creating a clear digital experience and working towards a solution that reflects the client's goals.
